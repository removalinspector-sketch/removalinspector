The FORMIN approach

Information before assumptions.

Legal issues are fact-sensitive. FORMIN aims to explain the vocabulary and process clearly, while showing readers where general information stops and professional advice begins.

Our editorial principles

  • Identify country, state, province or territory where relevant.
  • Prioritise legislation, courts and official government sources.
  • Show publication or review dates for legal guides.
  • Avoid guarantees about legal outcomes.
  • Correct errors and review changing laws and procedures.

Important legal notice

FORMIN provides general legal information for educational purposes only. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice, legal opinions, representation or attorney-client services. Reading this website does not create a lawyer-client relationship. Laws and procedures vary by jurisdiction and change over time; individual outcomes depend on specific facts.

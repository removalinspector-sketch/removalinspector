FREE CHECKER GET KIT R397 01 Why This Happens Credit provider didn't update status: System still shows “in arrears” / “adverse” even after settlement. Update failure is common. Settlement vs Paid-Up: Settled for less vs paid in full — bureau may still display adverse codes (e.g., “settled” with historical arrears). Batch reporting delay: Providers report monthly. Your final payment may miss the cycle. Data mismatch: Wrong balance, date, or account number kept on file. Educational insight only — verify on your own report. 02 What To Verify On Your Report Account Status Field Should say Paid-Up / Closed / Settled — not Arrears / Written-off if you paid. Balance vs Zero Paid-up accounts must reflect R0 balance. Any amount >0 is inaccurate. Payment History Codes Check last 12-24 months for adverse codes persisting after final payment date. Dates: Last Payment vs Updated Last payment date should align with settlement letter. Updated date should be recent. Duplicate Accounts Same debt listed twice inflates adverse count. Note both reference numbers. Not legal advice. Seek qualified advice if unsure. FAQ — Paid-Up Still Adverse Does paying remove adverse history automatically? No. Payment updates the status to paid-up / settled, but historical payment profile (e.g., months in arrears) can lawfully remain for the retention period. You can only dispute if the data is inaccurate — e.g., it still shows a balance >R0, wrong date, or status still ‘adverse’ after paid-up confirmation.

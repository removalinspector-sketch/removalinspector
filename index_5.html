The 20-Day Journey

From report to resolution

A clear timeline built on the National Credit Act — no legal jargon, no false promises.

Day 0

Decode & lodge

Identify the adverse listing, gather evidence, and send your Sec 72 dispute letter via bureau portal. Mask your ID to last 3 digits (POPIA).

Day 3

Acknowledgement check

Bureaus must acknowledge receipt. If no response, use our follow-up template. Log everything in your tracker.

Day 10

Follow-up & evidence

Send a polite follow-up with additional evidence (paid-up letters, prescription proof, Form 19). Keep the paper trail.

Day 20

Deadline & escalation

Sec 71(5) requires resolution within 20 business days. If unresolved, escalate to the NCR with our escalation pack.

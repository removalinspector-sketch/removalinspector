Start with your current debt-review status

Gather the documents and correspondence from your registered debt counsellor, including any relevant status notices. Do not assume that paying accounts automatically clears a debt-review flag. The process depends on your circumstances and the applicable legal requirements.

Understand Form 17.2 and Form 19 references

These forms relate to different stages or outcomes in the debt-review process. A guide can help you understand what a document is generally used for, but it cannot issue a clearance certificate or replace the registered debt counsellor, court process or other authorised step that may be required.

What the educational kit covers

The advertised kit includes a debt-review status audit, a clearance-certificate guide, Form 17.2 education and a bureau follow-up pack. Use it to organise questions, identify documents you may need to request and keep a record of communications with the relevant parties.

If the flag remains after your status changed

Compare the bureau entry with the latest valid documentation. Ask the responsible debt counsellor or relevant party to confirm what should be reported, then follow the bureau's official correction process if the information appears outdated or inaccurate. Keep case references and copies.

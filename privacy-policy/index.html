Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Removal Inspector (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit removalinspector.co.za, use the website checker or assistant, or contact us through WhatsApp or email. Removal Inspector provides educational information about South African credit profiles. We are not a credit bureau, law firm or registered debt counsellor.

Please protect your information. Do not send your identity number, passwords, bank-card details, full credit report or unredacted identity documents through the website chatbot or ordinary WhatsApp messages. Start with a general description of the issue. Share only what is necessary, through a channel we have confirmed is appropriate.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on how you use the website and which communication channel you choose, information may include:

  • Contact details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address or WhatsApp number.
  • The contents of enquiries and messages you send, including information you choose to type into the website assistant or checker.
  • Selections and answers you provide in the checker so it can display relevant general educational guidance.
  • Basic technical information generated when you visit the website, such as IP address, browser and device information, pages requested, timestamps and security logs. Hosting and security providers may process this information to deliver and protect the website.
  • Advertising and measurement information where advertising or analytics technologies are enabled.

Do not assume that a message or checker response is anonymous. Avoid entering sensitive personal or financial information unless it is genuinely necessary and we have explained the channel and purpose.

2. Why information may be used

Information may be used to operate and secure the website, respond to enquiries, provide the educational guidance you request, improve the checker and assistant, prevent misuse, maintain records of business communications, and meet legal obligations. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed for these purposes.

3. Chatbot and third-party services

Website assistant: When you send a message, the message and recent conversation context are sent to our Cloudflare-hosted assistant API, which uses Cloudflare Workers AI to generate a response. The assistant API sends recent messages for response generation but does not write message text or credit-report details into the assistant analytics database. Conversation messages are held in the active page session to maintain context and are not saved by the assistant widget to browser storage. The widget uses your browser’s local storage to remember your language choice, analytics choice and a random assistant session identifier; these values remain until cleared through your browser settings or changed by the website. The analytics database records limited event metadata only when you choose “Allow”, such as a random session identifier, language, event type, stage number, product category and timestamps. If you choose “No thanks”, these analytics events are not recorded; the assistant can still process your message to answer you. Your message is still transmitted to the AI service to provide the response.

The website may also use third-party hosting, security, messaging, analytics or advertising providers. Information submitted to a feature may be processed by that provider under its own terms and privacy practices. WhatsApp conversations are handled through WhatsApp and are subject to WhatsApp’s privacy terms. Website hosting and security providers may process technical request data.

The exact storage, access and retention settings can vary by provider and feature. Do not use the assistant or WhatsApp as a secure document-upload channel. Contact us if you need clarification about a particular interaction or want to request access to information you have supplied.

4. Cookies and Google advertising

This website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential website functions, security, analytics and advertising. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure and improve advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons and IP addresses as a result of ad serving on this website.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and how AdSense uses cookies. You can manage cookie settings through your browser. Blocking some cookies may affect website features or advertising.

Where applicable, we will use an appropriate consent mechanism for visitors in regions where consent is required for advertising cookies or personalised advertising.

5. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website or communications, where required by law, or where necessary to protect rights, safety and security. Some service providers may process information in countries outside South Africa. Where this occurs, applicable data-protection requirements should be considered and appropriate safeguards applied.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business needs, security and legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the systems used. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no website, messaging service or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), you may ask what personal information we hold about you, request access or correction, object to certain processing, or request deletion where legally appropriate. You may also raise a concern with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To make a privacy request, email support@removalinspector.co.za with the subject “Privacy Request”. Do not include your identity number or sensitive documents in your first email. We may need to verify your identity proportionately before responding.

8. Children

This website and its services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

How to use these estimates responsibly

Use the results as a starting point for your own planning, then compare them with your statements and the written terms from your lender or service provider. Do not share account numbers, identity numbers, passwords or banking credentials with anyone claiming they can improve a credit profile.

Removal Inspector provides general financial and credit-profile education. It is not a registered debt counsellor, lender, credit bureau or law firm, and does not make lending decisions or guarantee a credit outcome. For debt-review advice, contact your registered debt counsellor. For a credit-report dispute, follow the relevant bureau's official process.

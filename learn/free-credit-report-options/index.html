Credit reports · Guide 06

Where to get a free credit report in South Africa

You do not need to pay an unknown intermediary just to begin checking your credit information. Start with recognised credit bureaus and confirm their current consumer-access process directly.

Reviewed 19 November 2026 · General information

Start with the bureau itself

South African consumers can request access to their credit bureau information. Section 72(1)(b) of the National Credit Act provides for an inspection without charge once within any 12-month period, with additional access in specified circumstances. A bureau may charge for other inspections in accordance with applicable rules. Check each bureau's current terms, identity verification steps and delivery method.

Use official channels

Find the bureau through the Credit Bureau Association or the National Credit Regulator, then navigate to its own website. Avoid sponsored search results or social-media accounts that imitate a bureau. Check the domain spelling and privacy notice before submitting personal information.

Prepare for identity verification

A bureau may need to verify your identity before releasing a report. Follow its secure process and provide only the requested information. Never share online banking passwords, card PINs or one-time passwords. Do not upload a full credit report to a public website or an unknown WhatsApp number.

Check more than the score

When the report arrives, inspect your identity details, accounts, balances, payment history, enquiries, adverse classifications and any court-related entries. A score alone will not tell you whether an individual entry is correct.

Save a dated copy

Keep the date you requested the report and the date it was generated. If you later dispute an entry, the dated report helps identify exactly what you saw at the time. Different bureaus may hold different information or update at different times.

Remember: A free report does not guarantee a free credit score from every service, nor does checking your own report mean a lender will approve a future application.

Official starting points

  • Credit Bureau Association — bureau directory
  • National Credit Regulator
  • National Credit Act 34 of 2005

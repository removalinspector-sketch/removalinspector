Credit Repair Scams: Warning Signs Before You Pay

Learn how to spot guaranteed-removal promises, fake bureau contacts and pressure tactics when seeking help with a South African credit report.

People worried about a credit report may be targeted by services promising to “wipe”, “clean” or “unblacklist” them quickly for an upfront fee. Some providers may offer legitimate education or administrative support, but a promise to remove accurate information or guarantee a credit-score result is a serious warning sign.

Warning signs

  • Guaranteed deletion: nobody should promise that accurate, lawfully retained information will definitely be removed.
  • Pressure to pay immediately: be cautious if you are told an offer expires today or that you will lose your only chance.
  • Claims of special bureau access: ask for a clear explanation of the lawful process and who actually submits the dispute.
  • Requests for passwords or one-time PINs: never share banking credentials, bureau passwords or OTPs.
  • No written scope or refund terms: do not pay until you understand exactly what is being supplied and what is excluded.
  • Fake official branding: verify contact details on the regulator’s or bureau’s own website, not only through a forwarded message.

What you can do yourself

The NCA gives consumers rights to inspect and challenge credit information. You can request your report, identify an inaccurate entry, gather evidence and submit a dispute directly through the relevant bureau’s official channel. The NCR also publishes consumer information about disputed credit records.

Questions to ask before paying for help

  1. Is this education, document preparation, representation or a formal regulated service?
  2. What exact work will be completed, and what must I do myself?
  3. What is the full once-off or recurring cost?
  4. Will you promise an outcome? If so, ask how that promise is legally and factually justified.
  5. Will you provide a receipt, written terms and a privacy explanation?
  6. Can I verify the business and its contact details independently?

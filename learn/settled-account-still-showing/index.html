Account status · Guide 09

Paid account still showing on your credit report?

A settled account may remain visible in your credit history, but its balance and status should be examined separately. The key question is whether the information currently reported is accurate.

Reviewed 19 November 2026 · General information

Visible does not always mean incorrect

Paying an account does not necessarily mean every historical record disappears immediately. Credit reporting rules may allow certain accurate historical information to remain for prescribed periods. A paid-up status, current balance and historical payment profile are separate data points.

Get confirmation from the provider

Request a statement showing the account balance and a written settlement or paid-up confirmation. Check that it identifies the correct account and effective date. If a payment was made recently, ask when the provider expects to update the information it supplies to bureaus.

Compare the report carefully

  • Does the report show a current outstanding balance?
  • Does the status say unpaid, open, settled or paid-up?
  • Is the reporting date earlier than your final payment?
  • Does the account reference match your settlement document?
  • Is the historical payment profile being confused with the current balance?

Raise a specific dispute if needed

If the report still shows an incorrect balance or status after the provider has confirmed settlement, dispute that exact field with the bureau. Include the dated report and provider confirmation. Ask for the data provider to verify the current status. Keep your reference number and written outcome.

Do not pay twice to fix a reporting delay

If you have proof of settlement, do not make another payment simply because a report has not refreshed. First contact the provider through a verified channel and request an account reconciliation. If a genuine amount remains due, ask for an itemised explanation.

Important: A settlement letter can support a correction to the current balance or status. It does not automatically erase accurate historic payment information or a separate court record.

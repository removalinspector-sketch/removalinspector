Transparency and accountability

Editorial policy

Our aim is to publish accurate, useful and clearly qualified educational information—not to promise outcomes or disguise advertising as independent guidance.

Research and sources

For legal rights and regulatory processes, we prioritise primary sources such as legislation, regulations, government publications and regulator guidance. Secondary sources may provide context but do not replace the governing source. We link to sources so readers can verify important claims.

Plain-language explanations

We summarise complex topics for general understanding. A summary is not a substitute for the full law, official procedure or professional advice. We distinguish legal requirements from practical suggestions and examples where possible.

Review dates and updates

Guides display a review date indicating when the publisher last checked the page. A review date is not a guarantee that every legal development has been identified. We update material when we become aware of a material change or correction.

Corrections

Send the page URL, passage in question and a reliable supporting source to support@removalinspector.co.za. We assess correction requests and update material where appropriate.

Commercial content

Some pages may describe paid educational resources. Commercial content should be distinguishable from editorial guidance. Purchasing a kit does not affect the accuracy of credit-report information and cannot guarantee removal of an adverse entry.

Independence and limitations

Removal Inspector is not a law firm, debt counsellor, credit bureau or regulator. Articles are general education and do not create an advisory relationship. Obtain qualified advice for disputes, judgments, prescription questions and debt-review matters where the facts or legal consequences are uncertain.

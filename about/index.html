About the publication

About Removal Inspector

Removal Inspector is a South African educational resource that helps consumers understand credit-report information and the processes that may apply when information appears inaccurate or unclear.

What we publish

We publish plain-language explainers, checklists and educational tools covering credit reports, adverse information, debt review, prescription and consumer rights. Our aim is to explain the questions consumers should ask, the documents that may be relevant and the official sources where rules can be checked.

What we do not do

  • We are not a law firm, registered debt counsellor, credit bureau or government regulator.
  • We do not decide what a credit bureau should report.
  • We do not lodge disputes or court applications on behalf of consumers.
  • We do not promise removal of accurate information, score increases or credit approval.
  • Our free checker does not access bureau databases or inspect your credit report.

Commercial disclosures

We may offer paid educational templates or document kits. A purchase does not create a legal or debt-counselling relationship and does not guarantee that a template is appropriate for a particular case. Visitors should review the contents, limitations and price before deciding whether to purchase.

Contact

For editorial corrections or questions about published material, email support@removalinspector.co.za. Do not email passwords, one-time PINs or complete credit reports.

Editorial standards

Our approach to research, sources, corrections and review dates is explained in the Editorial Policy. For case-specific advice, consult an appropriately qualified professional or the relevant regulator.

General financial and credit-information education only. Removal Inspector is not a law firm, debt counsellor, credit bureau or regulator. We do not submit disputes on your behalf or guarantee removal, score improvement or credit approval.

The RANCE standard

Read the policy, not just the headline.

RANCE helps readers understand the language and mechanics of insurance so they can have better-informed conversations with licensed providers.

Our editorial principles

  • Explain premiums, exclusions and limits without promising outcomes.
  • Use official regulators and primary sources wherever possible.
  • Label country-specific content and review dates.
  • Disclose sponsorships and affiliate relationships clearly.
  • Correct errors and review changing information.

Important information

RANCE provides general insurance education only. It does not sell, arrange, underwrite or recommend insurance policies, provide regulated insurance advice, assess individual risks or handle claims. Policy coverage depends on the actual contract, applicable law and facts of each claim. Always read the policy wording and consult an appropriately licensed professional or regulator where necessary.

Advertising transparency

Any future sponsored material or affiliate relationship must be clearly identified and must not be confused with independent policy guidance.
